Programme structure

Three stages of study

Year 1

General Arts Qualifying

Indian thought and culture, the emergence of Buddhism, basic Buddhist teachings, Pali grammar and prescribed texts.

Year 2

BA Part I

Early and Mahayana Buddhist philosophy, South Asian culture, social institutions, Pali texts and commentarial literature.

Year 3

BA Part II

Buddhist social concepts, contemporary thought, East Asian culture, Buddhist art, canonical literature and analytical Pali study.

Academic progression

Your next step

Successful candidates may proceed to the one-year Master of Arts in Buddhist Studies.

Course Content First Year - General Arts Qualifying (GAQ)
1. Pre-Buddhist Indian Thought
2. Emergence of Buddhism and Basic Buddhist Teachings
3. Pre-Buddhist Indian Culture
4. Origin of Buddhism and Fundamentals of Culture
5. Introduction of Pali Grammar
6. Pali Prescribed Texts I

Second Year - Bachelor of Arts (BA) Part I
1. Philosophy of Early Buddhist Schools
2. Philosophy of Mahayana Buddhist Schools
3. Buddhist Culture in South Asia
4. The Buddhist Social Institutions
5. Pali Prescribed Text II
6. Pali Commentarial Literature

Third Year - Bachelor of Arts (BA) Part II
1. Buddhist Social Concepts
2. Buddhism and Contemporary Thought
3. Buddhist Culture in East Asia
4. Buddhist Art
5. History of Pali Canonical Literature
6. Pali Language / Analytical Study

Please refer to the course syllabus for details
