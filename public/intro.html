Course overview

Build a clear foundation in Buddhism

This course aims to introduce Buddhism for those with little or no knowledge of the subject, and who are interested to learn more. It also prepares participants who are interested to enrol for the Diploma in Buddhism course.

How much do you know about Buddhism?

Are you a knowledgeable Buddhist?

What is the essence of Buddhism?

Do you know how to practise Buddhism?

What you will learn

Course content

  1. The Freedom of Inquiry in Buddhism
  2. The Life of the Buddha
  3. The Three Characteristics of Existence
  4. The Four Noble Truths
  5. The Noble Eightfold Path
  6. Rebirth and Karma
  7. The Buddhist Texts
  8. How to Practise Buddhism
  9. The Different Buddhist Traditions
  10. The Meaning of Common Buddhist Rituals and Chanting
  11. Buddhist Meditation

Continue your studies

Your next step

After finishing this course, participants are encouraged to sign up for the one-year Diploma in Buddhism.
