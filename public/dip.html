Programme overview

Deepen your understanding of Buddhism

The Diploma in Buddhism is conducted by the Buddhist and Pali College of Singapore, affiliated to the Buddhist and Pali University of Sri Lanka. The programme combines foundational teachings with their historical, social and practical dimensions.

Applicants should be above 18 years of age.

Applicants should be able to read and write English.

Basic knowledge of Buddhism is an advantage.

What you will study

Curriculum

  1. Early Buddhism: Fundamental Teachings
  2. Buddhist Social Dimension
  3. Geographical Expansion of Buddhism
  4. Introduction to Buddhist Psychology and Counselling
  5. Pali Language and Literature
